# Accounts Receivable (AR) Manager — Job Description

**Industry:** Food Manufacturing
**Department:** Finance / Accounting
**Reports to:** Controller / Director of Finance
**Direct reports:** AR Clerks / Credit & Collections Specialists / Billing Coordinators
**FLSA status:** Exempt (Salaried)
**Location:** On-site (plant/corporate office) with limited remote flexibility

---

## Position Summary

The Accounts Receivable Manager leads the order-to-cash function for a food
manufacturing operation, ensuring that revenue is billed accurately, cash is
collected promptly, and customer accounts are managed in a way that protects
margins and supports continued growth. The role is central to working-capital
health in an industry defined by thin margins, high invoice volumes, perishable
inventory, and complex customer programs (deductions, promotions, slotting fees,
and trade spend). The AR Manager partners closely with Sales, Customer Service,
Logistics, and Plant Operations to resolve disputes quickly and keep product —
and cash — moving.

---

## Key Responsibilities

### Billing & Invoicing
- Oversee accurate and timely invoicing for finished goods, including
  catch-weight/variable-weight products, case/pallet pricing, and freight terms.
- Ensure invoices reconcile to purchase orders, bills of lading (BOL), and
  proof-of-delivery (POD) documentation.
- Manage EDI billing (810 invoices, 850/855 POs) and customer/retailer
  vendor-portal requirements for major grocery, club, foodservice, and
  distributor accounts.

### Cash Application & Collections
- Direct daily cash application, including lockbox, ACH, wire, and credit-card
  receipts, ensuring same-day posting accuracy.
- Drive collections to meet DSO and aging targets; escalate past-due accounts and
  negotiate payment plans where appropriate.
- Manage customer credit holds in coordination with Sales to balance revenue
  capture against collection risk.

### Deductions & Trade Spend (Critical for Food/CPG)
- Lead the deductions management process — chargebacks, shortages, spoilage/quality
  claims, OS&D (over, short & damaged), promotional allowances, and unauthorized
  deductions.
- Validate, code, and clear deductions; recover invalid deductions and document
  root causes to reduce repeat occurrences.
- Reconcile trade-promotion deductions against approved trade-spend accruals in
  partnership with Sales/Trade Marketing.

### Credit Management
- Establish and review customer credit limits using credit reports, references,
  and payment history.
- Monitor concentration risk among large retail/distributor customers and
  recommend credit insurance where warranted.

### Team Leadership
- Manage, coach, and develop the AR team; set goals, conduct reviews, and ensure
  proper segregation of duties.
- Establish and document AR policies, procedures, and internal controls.

### Reporting & Analysis
- Produce AR aging, DSO, bad-debt reserve, deduction recovery, and cash-forecast
  reporting for finance leadership.
- Support month-end close, including AR reconciliations, accruals, and audit
  requests.

### Cross-Functional & Continuous Improvement
- Partner with Customer Service, Logistics, and Quality to resolve disputes at the
  source.
- Identify and implement automation and process improvements (e.g., automated cash
  application, deduction workflows, customer self-service portals).

## Qualifications

### Required
- Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field (or equivalent
  experience).
- 5+ years of progressive accounts receivable experience, including 2+ years in a
  supervisory or management role.
- Direct experience with deductions/chargeback management in a food, beverage, or
  consumer-packaged-goods (CPG) environment.
- Strong understanding of order-to-cash, credit management, and GAAP revenue
  recognition.
- Proficiency with ERP systems (e.g., SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, NetSuite,
  or industry systems such as JustFood/Aptean) and advanced Excel.

### Preferred
- Experience with EDI and major retailer/distributor vendor portals.
- Familiarity with catch-weight/variable-weight billing and perishable-goods
  logistics.
- Experience with high-volume invoicing and large retail/club/foodservice
  customers.
- Exposure to deduction-management or trade-promotion software.

### Skills & Competencies
- Strong analytical and reconciliation skills with high attention to detail.
- Effective negotiator and communicator, comfortable working with customers and
  internal sales teams.
- Process-improvement mindset with the ability to manage high transaction volumes.
- Leadership presence and the ability to develop a high-performing team.

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)
- Aging (% current / past due by bucket)
- Deduction recovery rate and aged-deduction balance
- Bad-debt write-offs as a % of revenue
- Cash-application accuracy and timeliness
- Billing accuracy / invoice-error rate

## Working Conditions
- Office-based role within a manufacturing/corporate environment.
- Occasional plant-floor or warehouse interaction; periodic customer or trade
  travel.
- Month-end and quarter-end periods may require extended hours.
